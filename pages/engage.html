Collaborative Frameworks

Propose an Institutional Partnership

RAFA establishes formal evidence partnerships and knowledge co-production alliances under clearly defined public-purpose criteria.

Pathway A

Continental & Regional Organs

Partner with RAFA to co-convene sovereign ministerial roundtables, design cross-border regional value-chain corridors, or harmonize mineral trade rules under AfCFTA.

Contact: Use the briefing inquiry form
Pathway B

Research Institutes & Universities

Collaborate on empirical geological databases, peer review methodology notes, and co-publish open-access decision models with African university faculties.

Contact: Use the partnership inquiry form
Pathway C

Development Partners & Foundations

Discuss core institutional support, programmatic grants, or analytical tool capitalization protected by our strict governance firewalls.

Contact: Use the partnership inquiry form
Public-Purpose Partnership Boundary

All partnerships must comply with the RAFA Autonomy Charter. Commercial corporate entities may only participate in structured, open empirical evidence partnerships; RAFA does not provide commercial lobbying or represent private concessions.
