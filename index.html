Why RAFA

The transition is moving.
Africa should shape it.

Resources, industrial policy, climate rules, technology and geopolitics now move together. Africa’s capabilities are deep but dispersed. Too often, countries are left responding after the terms have already been set.

RAFA is an independent, pan-African strategic intelligence institution. We connect complementary strengths and turn knowledge into practical insight for the decisions that determine who captures value from global transitions.

What does “strategic intelligence” mean?

It is timely, independent and actionable insight on markets, technologies, regulation, finance and geopolitics — to help clarify trade-offs and support consequential decisions.

How is RAFA different from a traditional consultancy?

RAFA is designed as an independent, Africa-anchored, public-purpose institution. Partners may commission work, but do not predetermine or alter its conclusions.

Can we start a partnership conversation now?

Yes. Partnership conversations can begin with a shared strategic question, an intelligence gap, the right collaborators and a useful joint output.
